What is a Steps to Calories Calculator?

A steps to calories calculator helps you estimate how many calories you burn while walking or running. The calculation is based on your total steps, body weight, and an average calorie burn rate.

How Does It Work?

On average, a person burns about 0.04 to 0.06 calories per step. Heavier individuals burn slightly more calories per step than lighter individuals. This tool uses a scientifically accepted formula to give you a close estimate.

Why Track Calories Burned?

Tracking calories helps with weight loss, fitness goals, and maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Walking is one of the easiest and safest ways to stay active every day.
